Privacy Policy

AM Backgammon · Effective 2 August 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use AM Backgammon (the “App”), a backgammon game for iPhone and iPad published by Alexandru Matei Maier (“we”, “us”).

Information we collect directly

None. The App has no account system, no sign-in, and no registration. We do not ask for your name, email address, or contact details, and we do not operate servers that receive your personal information. Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Information collected by third parties

The App includes advertising software from third parties. When you use the App, these providers may automatically collect information from your device:

Unity Ads and Unity Ad Quality (ironSource) — both operated by Unity Technologies.

These are used to display advertisements and to measure and prevent fraudulent or low-quality ads.

Information these providers may collect includes:

  • Device advertising identifiers (IDFA / IDFV)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system version, language, and region
  • App usage and interaction data, including which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked
  • Diagnostic and performance data

This information is collected by those providers under their own privacy policies, not ours:

  • Unity privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity Ads / ironSource: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

How this information is used

Information collected by our advertising providers is used to select and deliver advertisements, measure their performance, limit how often the same ad is repeated, and detect invalid or fraudulent ad activity. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency. On iOS, apps must ask permission before tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared for that purpose.
  • Limit ad tracking. You can reset or disable your advertising identifier at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.
  • Deleting the App removes locally stored game data from your device.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data retention

We do not hold your personal information, because we do not collect it. Data collected by our advertising providers is retained according to their own retention policies, linked above.

International transfers

Our advertising providers operate globally and may process information in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States. Those transfers are governed by the providers’ own policies and safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Because we do not collect or store personal information ourselves, requests about advertising data should be directed to the relevant provider using the links above. You are welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to maier9900@gmail.com.
