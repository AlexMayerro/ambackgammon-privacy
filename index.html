Privacy Policy

AM Backgammon · Effective 2 August 2026

This policy explains what information is collected and processed when you use AM Backgammon (the “App”), a backgammon game for iPhone and iPad published by Alexandru Matei Maier (“we”, “us”).

The App offers single-player games against a computer opponent and online features including multiplayer matches, a friends list, and optional voice chat. Online features are provided using Unity Gaming Services, operated by Unity Technologies.

Your account

The App signs you in anonymously. You are not asked for an email address, a password, a phone number, or any real-world identity, and there is no registration screen.

Unity Authentication issues a persistent Player ID that identifies your account across sessions and devices so your progress, statistics, and friends list remain yours. This ID is a random identifier, not a name.

Information you choose to provide

  • Display name. You may set a name shown to other players — in matches, in friends lists, and in voice chat if you enable it. Choose a name you are comfortable showing publicly; do not use your full legal name or contact details.
  • Friend connections. Adding a friend, or accepting a request, shares your display name and online status with that player.

The virtual currency in the App has no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for money.

Voice chat

Voice chat is off by default and never starts on its own. It is only activated when you deliberately turn it on during a match.

If you enable voice chat, the App uses Vivox (Unity Technologies) and iOS will ask for microphone permission. While you are in a voice channel your audio is transmitted to the other players in that match, and your display name is shown to them. Turning voice chat off, or denying microphone permission, stops this entirely — the rest of the game is unaffected.

Advertising

The App shows advertisements through Unity Ads and uses Unity Ad Quality (ironSource) to detect fraudulent or low-quality ads. These providers may collect your device advertising identifier, IP address, device characteristics, and records of which ads were shown or clicked, under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity player and app user privacy policy
  • Unity privacy policy

On iOS, apps must ask permission before tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared for that purpose. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • We do not collect payment or financial information.
  • We do not run a third-party analytics service in the App.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not record or store voice chat audio.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. It is processed by the service providers named above so that the App can work — matchmaking, saving progress, showing ads. We may disclose information if required by law, or to investigate abuse, fraud, or violations of our rules.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App includes online multiplayer, a friends list, optional voice chat with other players, and advertising. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Retention and deletion

Account data — your Player ID, saved settings, statistics, wallet balance, and friends list — is kept while your account is active, so that deleting and reinstalling the App does not lose your progress. To request deletion of your account and its associated data, email us at the address below. Data held by advertising providers is retained under their own policies, linked above.

International transfers

Unity Gaming Services and our advertising providers operate globally and may process information in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States. Those transfers are governed by the providers’ own safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, contact us at the address below and tell us your in-game display name so we can locate your account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when we do.
